Arguments

Python supports positional, keyword, default, positional-only and keyword-only styles.

Interview focus

Know that mutable default arguments can be dangerous.

*args & **kwargs

*args collects extra positional arguments and **kwargs collects extra keyword arguments.

Interview focus

Explain unpacking with * and ** too.

Scope & LEGB

Name lookup generally follows Local, Enclosing, Global and Built-in scopes.

Interview focus

Know global and nonlocal before using them.

Lambda

Lambda creates a small anonymous function expression.

Interview focus

Use it for simple expressions, not complex multi-step logic.

Closures

An inner function can retain access to names from its enclosing scope.

Interview focus

Closures are the foundation of many decorators.

Recursion

A function calls itself until a base case is reached.

Interview focus

Know recursion limits and why iteration is often safer in Python.
