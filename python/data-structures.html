Lists

Ordered, mutable sequences with fast append and flexible indexing.

Interview focus

Explain why inserting at the beginning can be expensive.

Tuples

Ordered, immutable sequences.

Interview focus

Know immutability, unpacking and when tuples are useful.

Sets

Unordered collections of unique hashable elements with fast membership on average.

Interview focus

Explain why a list cannot be a set element.

Dictionaries

Hash-based mappings from hashable keys to values.

Interview focus

Know key uniqueness, lookup behavior and insertion-order guarantees in modern Python.

Comprehensions

Compact syntax for constructing lists, sets and dictionaries.

Interview focus

Copying

Assignment shares a reference; shallow copy duplicates the outer container; deep copy recursively copies nested objects.

Interview focus

Be ready with a nested-list example.

Quick interview recap

  1. Lists: Explain why inserting at the beginning can be expensive.
  2. Tuples: Know immutability, unpacking and when tuples are useful.
  3. Sets: Explain why a list cannot be a set element.
  4. Dictionaries: Know key uniqueness, lookup behavior and insertion-order guarantees in modern Python.
  5. Comprehensions: Prefer readability over deeply nested comprehensions.
