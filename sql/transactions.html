🔒 Transactions & Concurrency

Keep related changes correct when failures and concurrent users are involved.

Start Learning ↓

Transactions

A transaction groups operations into a unit with COMMIT, ROLLBACK and often SAVEPOINT.

Interview focus

Use transactions for multi-step operations that must succeed or fail together.

ACID

Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation and Durability describe reliable transactional behavior.

Interview focus

Give a bank-transfer example and distinguish isolation from consistency.

Isolation levels

READ UNCOMMITTED, READ COMMITTED, REPEATABLE READ and SERIALIZABLE trade concurrency against anomalies; behavior varies by engine.

Interview focus

Know dirty reads, non-repeatable reads and phantom reads.

Locks & MVCC

Databases coordinate concurrent work with locks and/or multiversion concurrency control.

Interview focus

Explain why reads may not always block writes in MVCC systems.

Deadlocks

Transactions can wait cyclically for resources held by each other.

Interview focus

Keep transactions short, access resources consistently and retry victims safely.

Lost updates

Concurrent writers can overwrite each other without coordination.

Interview focus

Explain optimistic version columns or appropriate locking.

Quick interview recap

  1. Transactions: Use transactions for multi-step operations that must succeed or fail together.
  2. ACID: Give a bank-transfer example and distinguish isolation from consistency.
  3. Isolation levels: Know dirty reads, non-repeatable reads and phantom reads.
  4. Locks & MVCC: Explain why reads may not always block writes in MVCC systems.
  5. Deadlocks: Keep transactions short, access resources consistently and retry victims safely.
